Privacy Policy

Stilla is built to be private: no account, no ads, no tracking. This policy explains the little data that is involved and the rights you have.

Who is responsible

The controller responsible under the GDPR is Shaker Badal Shukur Shukur. Contact: vikingershaker@gmail.com.

No account, no tracking

You can use Stilla without an account. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

Stored only on your device

Your goals, sessions, favorites, mood check-ins, reminder times and settings are stored locally on your iPhone and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them, and you can also reset them at any time in Settings.

Audio and content

All meditations, stories and sounds are included in the app, so playing them does not contact any server.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. If you start a free trial, we schedule one local reminder shortly before it ends. No server is involved, and you can turn notifications off in your iPhone settings at any time.

Purchases

Payments for Stilla Plus are processed by Apple. We never see your name or payment details. To verify your subscription and restore it on your devices we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) as our processor. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status from Apple, and technical data such as app version, operating system, device type, country or App Store region and IP address. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Data may be processed in the United States; such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. RevenueCat's privacy policy: www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple

If you agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you reset it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as they are needed to provide your subscription and to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object. Write to vikingershaker@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and in the app with a new date.
