Support

Need help with Stilla? Email vikingershaker@gmail.com and you'll hear back, usually within two days.

Restore Stilla Plus

Open Settings → Restore purchases in Stilla, or tap Restore purchases on the Stilla Plus screen. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Manage or cancel your subscription

In Stilla: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stilla. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders not arriving?

Turn on reminders in Stilla under Settings, then make sure notifications are allowed for Stilla in your iPhone settings.

Your data

Your progress, favorites and check-ins stay on your iPhone. Settings → Reset app deletes them.
